About Breakout

Breakout · Estonia, EU

Who we are

Breakout is a music promotion platform based in Estonia, EU. We help independent artists, labels, producers and managers grow their presence on Spotify and SoundCloud — from plays, followers and saves to playlist placements and full promotion packages.

Great music doesn't always get the reach it deserves. Streaming algorithms reward tracks that already have momentum — and getting that first momentum is the hardest part of an independent artist's career. That's the problem Breakout exists to solve: we give your release the initial push that lets the platform's own algorithm pick it up.

What we do

Every campaign is delivered through organic, platform-safe methods — no bots, no fake engagement that puts your profile at risk. You choose a service and quantity, we handle delivery, and you can track the results directly on your platform dashboard. Campaigns start within hours of payment and deliver gradually, the way natural growth looks.

  • Spotify — plays, followers, monthly listeners, saves and curated playlist placements.
  • SoundCloud — plays, likes, reposts, followers and comments.
  • Bundles — packaged campaigns that combine several services for a bigger combined effect.

Numbers that speak for themselves

  • 10,000+ campaigns completed
  • 5,000,000+ total streams delivered
  • 1,000+ satisfied artists
  • 0 account penalties

How we work with you

Support is available 24/7 at breakoutmusicsupport@gmail.com — we aim to answer every inquiry within 24 hours. Payments are processed by PCI-certified providers, and every order is covered by our Refund Policy. If something about your campaign doesn't go as planned, we make it right.

Have questions before ordering? Start with the FAQ or contact us directly — for custom campaigns, larger budgets or label partnerships, write us with details about your release and goals and we'll put together a personalized plan.
